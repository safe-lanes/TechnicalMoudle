Task: Redesign and implement Monthly Maintenance Summary using opening snapshot, monthly movement, and closing snapshot
Objective
Redesign the Monthly Maintenance Summary report so that it becomes a true monthly control report, not a live mixed summary.

The report must show:

Opening snapshot at the start of the month

Monthly movement during the month

Closing snapshot at the end of the month

This is the only way to get a true monthly maintenance analysis. The monthly summary should be based on the actual PMS work order lifecycle buckets already used in the system:

Planned

Due

Overdue

Postponed

Unplanned

Pending Approval

Completed

Core business requirement
At the start of each month, the system must capture the exact count and supporting records of all work orders falling into the above sections.
At the end of each month, the system must again capture the exact count and supporting records of all work orders in those same sections.

Then the Monthly Maintenance Summary report must use these month-boundary snapshots plus in-month movement to present a proper management report.

This is a business-correctness implementation, not just a cosmetic UI change.
