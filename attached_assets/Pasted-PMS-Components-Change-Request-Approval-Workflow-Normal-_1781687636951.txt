PMS – Components – Change Request Approval Workflow (Normal Equipment)

When a Vessel user raises a Modify PMS Request for an Equipment/Component, the system shall first determine whether the equipment is classified as a Normal Equipment.

The system shall identify the equipment as a Normal Equipment when:

Component Criticality = No
Class Item = No

Once the equipment is identified as a Normal Equipment, the system shall verify the Approval Workflow configuration under:

PMS → Components → Change Request → Normal Equipment

The system shall then determine whether the approval workflow is configured for:

Level 1 only
Level 2 only
Both Level 1 and Level 2

Based on the configuration, the request shall be routed to the designated approver(s).

Approval Access

Only the configured approver(s) shall have permission to approve or reject the request.

Users who are not part of the configured approval level shall have Read-Only access and shall not be allowed to approve or reject the request.

Additionally, only the configured approver(s) shall receive the request in the Operations Dashboard – Pending Approval KPI/Card.

The request shall also be available for review and approval from the Modify PMS Request section.

Scenario 1 – Only Level 1 Configured
The request shall be routed to the Level 1 Approver.
The Level 1 Approver shall receive the request in the Pending Approval section.
The Level 1 Approver may review and either approve or reject the request.
Upon approval, the requested changes shall be processed and synchronized to the Vessel.

Workflow:

Vessel User → Modify PMS Request → Level 1 Approval → Synchronization to Vessel

Scenario 2 – Only Level 2 Configured
The request shall be routed directly to the Level 2 Approver.
The Level 2 Approver shall receive the request in the Pending Approval section.
The Level 2 Approver may review and either approve or reject the request.
Upon approval, the requested changes shall be processed and synchronized to the Vessel.

Workflow:

Vessel User → Modify PMS Request → Level 2 Approval → Synchronization to Vessel

Scenario 3 – Both Level 1 and Level 2 Configured
The request shall first be routed to the Level 1 Approver.
Only the Level 1 Approver shall be able to approve or reject the request at this stage.
Level 2 Approvers shall have Read-Only access until Level 1 approval is completed.
Once approved by Level 1, the request shall be forwarded to the Level 2 Approver.
The Level 2 Approver shall then review and approve or reject the request.
The requested changes shall be processed only after Level 2 approval is completed.
The approved changes shall then be synchronized to the Vessel.

Workflow:

Vessel User → Modify PMS Request → Level 1 Approval → Level 2 Approval → Synchronization to Vessel

Rejection Handling
Level 1 Rejection
The request status shall be updated to Rejected.
The workflow shall be terminated immediately.
The request shall not proceed to Level 2.
No changes shall be applied.
The Rejected status shall be synchronized to the Vessel.
Level 2 Rejection
The request status shall be updated to Rejected.
The workflow shall be terminated.
No changes shall be applied.
The Rejected status shall be synchronized to the Vessel.
Expected Result
The system shall identify Normal Equipment based on:
Component Criticality = No
Class Item = No
Approval routing shall follow the configured workflow settings.
Only configured approvers shall receive Pending Approval requests.
Non-configured users shall have Read-Only access.
Changes shall be applied only after all configured approval levels have approved the request.
If any approver rejects the request, the request shall be marked as Rejected, no changes shall be applied, and the rejection status shall be synchronized to the Vessel.
