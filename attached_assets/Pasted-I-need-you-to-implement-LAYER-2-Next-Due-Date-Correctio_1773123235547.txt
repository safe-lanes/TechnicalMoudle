BACKGROUND / PROBLEM STATEMENT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Currently, when a work order is completed, the next due date is calculated 
using the formula:

  nextDueDate = completionDate + frequencyInterval   ← THIS IS WRONG

This is the root cause of schedule manipulation. If a crew member enters 
a backdated or delayed completion date, the entire future schedule shifts 
forward permanently. Skipped cycles are never recovered.

EXAMPLE OF THE PROBLEM:
- Job frequency: Every 1 Week
- Original scheduled due date (nextDueDate on work order): 01-Jan-2026
- Crew completes on 31-Jan-2026 (1 month late, 3 cycles missed)
- Current system calculates: next due = 31-Jan-2026 + 1 week = 07-Feb-2026
- RESULT: The schedule has permanently drifted 1 month forward. 
  The 3 missed cycles are gone. Future maintenance is now always 1 month late.

THE FIX:
Instead, the next due date must be calculated from the original 
scheduled due date:

  nextDueDate = originalScheduledDueDate + frequencyInterval  ← CORRECT

Using the same example:
- Original due date: 01-Jan-2026
- Frequency: 1 Week
- CORRECT next due = 01-Jan-2026 + 1 week = 08-Jan-2026
- Even if completed on 31-Jan-2026, the schedule SNAPS BACK to where 
  it should be. The next job is due 08-Jan-2026 (which means it's 
  immediately overdue again, correctly).

This ensures the maintenance schedule is always anchored to the 
original planned dates, not to whenever the crew decides to complete 
the job.

IMPORTANT DEFINITIONS

"Original Scheduled Due Date" = the nextDueDate field on the work_orders 
table AT THE TIME of completion. This is the date the job was SUPPOSED 
to be done. It is already stored in the database.

"Completion Date" = the dateOfCompletion entered by the user in the 
completion form (Part B, Section B2.1). This is the date the job was 
ACTUALLY done (or claims to be done).

"Frequency Interval" = frequencyValue + frequencyUnit from the jobs table.
Example: frequencyValue=1, frequencyUnit="Weeks" means add 1 week.
