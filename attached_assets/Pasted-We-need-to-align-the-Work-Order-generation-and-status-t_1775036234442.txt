Business rules to be followed
Below are the core business rules that the PMS should follow.

1) Planned Work Order generation rule
Work Orders must be generated in advance before they become due.

Required rule:

For date-based jobs, Work Orders should be generated 30 days in advance from the due date.

For running-hours-based jobs, Work Orders should be generated 720 hours in advance from the due point.

These generated Work Orders must appear under the “Planned” tab in the Work Order sub-module.

2) Due transition rule
Each vessel has a Lead Time setting.

Required rule:

Once the job enters the vessel’s configured Lead Time window, the Work Order must become Due.

At that point, it must move from the Planned tab to the Due tab.

This means:

Planned = generated early but not yet inside due action window.

Due = now within operational action window defined by vessel Lead Time.

3) Grace Period rule
Each vessel has a Grace Period setting.

Required rule:

After the actual Due Date has passed, the Work Order should not immediately move to the Overdue tab if the vessel still has active grace period days/hours remaining.

During that grace period, the Work Order must still be visibly marked with the existing “Grace P” marker, as presently shown in the system.

Important:

During grace period, it should be treated as past due date but still within grace allowance.

The existing visible Grace P marker behavior should be preserved conceptually.

4) Overdue transition rule
Required rule:

Once the configured Grace Period has fully expired, the Work Order must become Overdue.

At that point it must appear under the “Overdue” tab.

This is the final transition after the grace window ends.

Expected status lifecycle
The intended lifecycle should therefore be:

Work Order generated

30 days before due date for date-based jobs

720 hours before due point for running-hours-based jobs

Appears in Planned

Work Order enters Lead Time window

As per vessel-specific Lead Time setting

Moves to Due

Due date passes

If grace period exists and is still active, show with Grace P marker

Do not move to final Overdue yet

Grace period expires

Move to Overdue

Show under Overdue tab
