ANSWER (verbatim):
1. Select the checklist question and click its **Attachments** icon.
2. Add the file by clicking **Browse** and selecting it, or by dragging and dropping it into the upload area.
3. Multiple attachments may be added to the question. Keep the combined size of files in one upload at **20 MB or less**.
4. Save periodically, especially when uploading multiple or large files. The combined size of all attachments between two consecutive **Save** actions or Offline-to-Online synchronizations should not exceed **150 MB**.
5. After uploading, the attachment appears under the corresponding question in the **Uploaded** section. You can **view** or **download** it.
6. To remove it, click its **delete** icon.
