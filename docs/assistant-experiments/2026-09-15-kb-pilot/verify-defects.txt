ANSWER (verbatim):
The survey-specific procedure is **not covered** in the Technical documentation. It only says to refer to the Certificates sub-sub-module; this does not establish whether the survey process is identical or different.

The documented certificate attachment process is:

1. Select the vessel from the **Vessel** dropdown.
2. Click the **Attachment** icon for the certificate record.
3. Click the upload area and select a **PDF, JPG, or PNG** file.
4. Use **View** to preview or **Delete** to remove an attachment.
5. Click **Done** to save the changes.
