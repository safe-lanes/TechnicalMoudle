FULL ANSWER:
No—**on the ship, planned work orders are created automatically by the PMS**. The system scans once daily and generates them from each job’s frequency and next due date; no user action is required.

**In the office**, planned work orders are not generated automatically. A **Sail Admin** must:

1. Open the Work Orders screen for the vessel.
2. Click **Generate Now**.

The vessel’s **office work-order generation switch must be ON**. Normal sign-in and vessel access also apply.

The draft code-derived guidance also supports two other creation paths: generating a work order for one job through **Components → Part C → Generate WO**, or creating an **unplanned work order** through **Work Orders → + Unplanned W.O**.

FULL ANSWER:
No—planned work orders do not always require you to create them yourself. The draft code-derived guidance supports these planned-work-order paths:

1. **Automatic ship generation:** On the ship, PMS creates scheduled work orders automatically once a day from each job’s frequency and next due date. No user action is required.

2. **Office “Generate Now”:** In the office, automatic generation does not run by itself. A **Sail Admin** can select one vessel on the Work Orders screen and click **Generate Now**, but only when that vessel’s **office work-order generation** switch is ON. The switch is set in **Lead Time & Grace Period Settings** and is off by default. A job with an active work order cannot receive another one.

3. **Per-job “Generate WO”:** Open **Components**; in the office, select the vessel; open the component, go to **Part C**, click **Generate WO**, and choose **Planning**, **Breakdown**, or **Other**. No special role check applies, but normal sign-in and vessel access are required. In the office, the vessel switch must be ON; the job must be active and must not already have an active work order.

The published June manuals describe per-job/unplanned creation and work-order planning, but do not describe automatic planned generation or **Generate Now**. The additional details above come from draft, code-derived guidance inspected at revision **cf5241ad**.

FULL ANSWER:
The excerpts support three ways to create a new work order:

1. **Planned/scheduled work order**
   - On the **Ship**, the system creates work orders automatically once a day from each job’s frequency and next due date; no user action is required.
   - In the **Office**, a **Sail Admin** can click **Generate Now** on the Work Orders screen for one vessel, provided that vessel’s **office work-order generation switch is ON**. The switch is set in **Lead Time & Grace Period Settings** and is off by default.
   - The June manuals do not describe this planned-generation process; it comes from draft, code-derived guidance.

2. **Generate a work order for one job**
   - Sign in with access to the vessel. No special role is required.
   - In the **Office**, select the vessel and ensure its **office work-order generation switch is ON**. This switch is not required on the Ship.
   - Go to **Components** and open the required component.
   - Open **Part C** and click **Generate WO**.
   - Select the reason: **Planning**, **Breakdown**, or **Other**.
   - The job must be active and must not already have an active work order.

3. **Create an unplanned work order**
   - Sign in and have access to the relevant vessel. No special role or switch is required.
   - Go to the **Work Orders** sub-sub-module.
   - **Office only:** select the vessel from the **Vessel** dropdown. On the Ship, the vessel is fixed.
   - Click **+ Unplanned W.O**.
   - Enter the required job details in **Part A**, then click **Save** to store the form as a draft.
   - Complete **Part B – Work Completion Record**, then click **Submit Work Order** to create the work order.

FULL ANSWER:
The excerpts support three ways to create a new work order. The first two are described in draft, code-derived guidance; the unplanned process is also documented in the published manuals.

1. **Planned work order**
   - **On Ship:** The system creates work orders automatically once per day, based on each job’s frequency and next due date. No user action is required.
   - **In Office:** A **Sail Admin** can open the Work Orders screen and click **Generate Now** for one vessel, but only when that vessel’s **office work-order generation** switch is ON. The switch is set in **Lead Time & Grace Period Settings** and is OFF by default.
   - The June published manuals do not describe automatic ship generation or the Office **Generate Now** process.

2. **Generate a work order for one job**
   1. In **Office**, select the vessel under **Components**.
   2. Open the component.
   3. Go to **Part C**.
   4. Click **Generate WO**.
   5. Select the reason: **Planning**, **Breakdown**, or **Other**.
   - No special role is required, but the user must be signed in and have vessel access.
   - In the Office, the vessel’s **office work-order generation** switch must be ON.
   - The job must be active and must not already have an active work order.

3. **Unplanned work order**
   1. Go to the **Work Orders** sub-sub-module.
   2. **Office:** Select the vessel from the **Vessel** dropdown. On **Ship**, the vessel is fixed, so this selection is not required.
   3. Click **+ Unplanned W.O**.
   4. Enter the required job details in **Part A**, then click **Save** to store the form as a draft.
   5. Complete **Part B** and click **Submit Work Order** to create the work order.
   - No special role or switch is required; normal sign-in and vessel access still apply.

The planned-generation and per-job procedures are marked as draft, code-derived guidance based on application revision `cf5241ad`, not published manuals.
