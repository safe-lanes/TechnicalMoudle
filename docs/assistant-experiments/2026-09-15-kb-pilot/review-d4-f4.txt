* Click on the ‘Admin’ sub-module. (Ref Figure 12)

* Click the ‘Ship’s Certificates’ sub-submodule to view certificate details; the ‘Master’ tab is displayed by default. (Ref Figure 12)

* Upon entering the ‘Ship’s Certificates’ sub-submodule, the system automatically opens the ‘Master’ tab by default, allowing users to view all certificate details:

 * **Master Tab**: The Master tab serves as the central repository for all certificate master records available in the system. It allows administrators to define and maintain certificate types, categories, groups, regulatory references, and certificate labels. This view establishes the standard certificate structure that can be applied across companies and vessels. (Ref Figure 12)

 * **Company Tab**: The Company tab displays certificate records configured for individual companies. It allows administrators to review company-specific certificate requirements, certificate labels, regulatory references, and company group assignments. This view helps ensure that the appropriate certificate masters are assigned and maintained at the company level. (Ref Figure 12)

 * **Vessel Tab**: The Vessel tab displays certificate records applicable

ANSWER (verbatim):
For an authorized Office user:

1. Click the **Admin** sub-module.
2. Click **Ship’s Certificates**. The **Master** tab opens by default.
3. Click **Edit**.
4. Click **New**. The system adds an editable row at the bottom with an auto-generated **Master ID**.
5. Enter the required details: **Certificate Name, Category, Group, Requirement/Reference, Applicable to Company,** and **Certificate Label**. Mandatory fields must be completed.
6. Click the **Save** icon.
7. Choose **Yes, Reorder** to move the certificate to the selected Category and Group, or **No, Keep at End** to leave it at the end of the table.

The Master list contains system-defined certificates that users cannot delete.

**Source:** Technical - Cert. & Surveys User Manual For Office_Sail Admin_R2_08.06.2026, sections 1.2.1.1 “How to Access the Ship’s Certificates” (p.13), 1.2.1.4 “How to Add a New Master Certificate” (p.17), and technical user manual steps (p.18).

ANSWER (verbatim):
For **Stores**, the manuals show a separate entry path, but direct you to the Spares bulk-update procedure for the remaining steps.

1. **Stores bulk update — Vessel**
   1. Open the **Store** sub-sub-module.
   2. Click **+ Bulk Updates Store**.
   3. The manual then directs you to the **Spares** sub-sub-module and its procedure. It also says this process applies to **Stores, Lubes, Chemicals, or Others**, and to the **Inventory, Location, or History** tabs.
   
   The vessel documentation does not provide the subsequent transaction-entry steps in the Stores section.

2. **Stores bulk update — Office**
   1. Open the **Store** sub-sub-module.
   2. Click **+ Bulk Updates Store**.
   3. Follow the Spares bulk-update instructions:
      1. Select **Consume** or **Receive**.
      2. Enter the required details and update quantities in the table.
      3. Click **Save Updates**.

So, Stores has a different starting button and sub-module, but the Office documentation directs the remaining process to the Spares bulk-update instructions. The excerpts do not state any role requirement.

The other supported bulk-update process is **Spares**: open **Spares**, click **Bulk Update Spares**, select the transaction mode, enter/update details, and save the updates.
