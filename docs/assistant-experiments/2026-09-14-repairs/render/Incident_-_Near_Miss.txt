## 2. NEAR MISS - OVERVIEW

* Upon entering the Near Miss sub-module, the *All Reports* screen is displayed. (See Figure 3)

  1. Click **Filters** to access filtering options.

  2. Select the required **Time Period**.

  3. Click **Apply** to filter the records or **Clear** to reset the filters.

<u>Figure 3</u>

* Click the **Export** icon to download the report list. (See Figure 4)

* Click **Show Pending Actions** to view records with pending actions. (See Figure 4)
