# 1.1.5 CONDITION OF CLASS (COC)

**Introduction**

* The '**Condition of Class (COC)**' sub-sub-module provides a centralized view of all CoC-related defects across the fleet. It enables users to create, monitor, search, filter, and manage defect records, track their status and target dates, record corrective actions, and perform activities such as viewing, editing, verifying, linking, and exporting defect information to support class compliance and vessel safety.

## 1.1.5.1 HOW TO ACCESS CONDITION OF CLASS (COC)

* Click on the 'COC' sub—submodule to view the all COC defect records. (Ref Figure 29)
