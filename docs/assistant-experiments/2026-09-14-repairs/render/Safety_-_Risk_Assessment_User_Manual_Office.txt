## 6.3 COMPLETING OFFICE RA - PART A (OVERVIEW)

I. Enter the required details under **A.1 Basic Information**.

II. Select the applicable **RA Type** using the available radio buttons.

III. Complete the task-related information under **A.2 Task Related Information** and **A.3 Alternative Method of Carrying Out the Task**, as applicable.

IV. Click **Save** to store the **Risk Assessment** details.
(See Figure 13)

Figure 13

☞ A **Report ID** is generated automatically once **Part–A** has been saved.

☞ **Part B** remains locked until Part A has been completed and saved.

## 6.4 COMPLETING OFFICE RA - PART B (HAZARD ASSESSMENT)

* Fill in all required fields in **Part—B** accordingly.

➢ <u>**Method 1**: Add Hazards from Database. (See Figure 14)</u>

I. Click **+ Add Hazards From Database** and select the required hazards and associated possible incidents.*(See Figure 15)*

II. Click **AUTOFILL** to automatically populate the mapped Threats and Existing Controls for all hazards. *(See Figure 16)*

III. Add Initial and final Risk ratings manually, as applicable.

IV. Click **Auto Arrange Hazards**, to automatically rearrange the selected hazards.
