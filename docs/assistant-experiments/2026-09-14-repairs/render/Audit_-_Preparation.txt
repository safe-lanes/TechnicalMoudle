## 4.4.4 View Repeated Observations

* <u>Click the **Repeated** indicator under a question to view how many times observations for the</u> <u>selected **VIQ reference/question** have been recorded.</u>

* <u>A **Repeated Observations** pop-up window is displayed.</u>

* <u>Use the **Company**, **Vessel**, or **Pool** tabs to view repeated observations from the respective</u> <u>source.</u>

  * <u>**Company** – Displays all previous observations recorded against the selected question across</u> <u>the company's fleet.</u>

  * <u>**Vessel** – Displays previous observations recorded against the selected question for the</u> <u>selected vessel only.</u>

  * <u>**Pool** – Displays observations for the selected question available in the SAIL Pool.</u>

* <u>Click the **Export** icon to export the displayed observation data.</u>
