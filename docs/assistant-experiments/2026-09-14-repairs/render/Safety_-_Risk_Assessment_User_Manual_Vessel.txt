## 8. HOW TO FILL VESSEL RISK ASSESSMENT FORM – PART B

* Fill in all required fields in **Part—B** accordingly.

⮚ <u>**Method 1**</u>: **Add Hazards from Database.** (*See Figure 13*)

I. Click **+ Add Hazards From Database** and select the required *hazards* and associated *possible incidents*.(*See Figure 14*)

II. Click **AUTOFILL** to automatically populate the mapped *Threats* and *Existing Controls* for all hazards. (*See Figure 15*)

III. Add Initial and final Risk ratings manually, as applicable.

IV. Click **Auto Arrange Hazards**, to automatically rearrange the selected hazards.
